What is Display & Video 360?

Display & Video 360 (DV360) is Google's integrated platform for planning, buying, and measuring digital advertising across display, video, audio, and native formats. It combines the capabilities of DoubleClick Bid Manager, Studio, and Campaign Manager into a single platform.

Key Features

FeatureDescription
Programmatic BuyingReal-time bidding across Google's exchange and third-party exchanges
Creative ToolsBuilt-in creative studio for rich media and dynamic creative optimization
Audience TargetingFirst-party, third-party, and Google audience segments
Cross-Channel PlanningPlan campaigns across display, video, audio, and TV
MeasurementIntegrated with Google Analytics 4 and Campaign Manager 360

Interface Overview

DV360's interface is organized into several main sections:

1. Advertisers & Campaigns

Navigate the hierarchy: Partner → Advertiser → Campaign → Insertion Order → Line Item

2. Inventory

Browse and secure ad inventory through open auctions, private deals, and programmatic guaranteed.

3. Audiences

Create and manage audience segments for targeting.

4. Creatives

Upload, create, and manage ad creatives including display, video, and native formats.

5. Insights & Reporting

Access performance data, attribution reports, and audience insights.

When to Use DV360

  • Large-scale brand awareness campaigns
  • Cross-channel programmatic buying
  • Access to premium inventory via programmatic guaranteed deals
  • Advanced creative needs (rich media, dynamic creative)
