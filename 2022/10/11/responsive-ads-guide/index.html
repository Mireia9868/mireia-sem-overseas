What Are Responsive Search Ads?

Responsive search ads (RSAs) let you provide multiple headlines and descriptions. Google automatically tests different combinations to learn which perform best, then shows the most effective combinations to users.

Key Features

  • Up to 15 headlines (30 characters each)
  • Up to 4 descriptions (90 characters each)
  • Automatic testing: Google rotates combinations and optimizes over time
  • Pin headlines: Pin specific headlines to positions 1, 2, or 3 for brand control
  • Ad strength indicator: Real-time feedback on ad quality (Poor, Average, Good, Excellent)

Understanding Ad Strength

RatingMeaningAction
PoorNot enough variety or relevanceAdd more headlines and descriptions
AverageSome variety but needs improvementAdd unique content, avoid duplicates
GoodGood variety and relevanceConsider adding more unique content
ExcellentHigh variety, relevance, and uniquenessOptimal — monitor performance

Best Practices

  1. Provide all 15 headlines: More options = more combinations tested
  2. Make each headline unique: Avoid variations of the same message
  3. Include keywords: At least 30% of headlines should contain target keywords
  4. Add CTAs: Include action-oriented headlines ("Buy Now," "Get a Quote")
  5. Use dynamic content: Keyword insertion and countdown customizers
  6. Don't pin everything: Pinning limits Google's ability to test combinations
Related: RSAs work best with ad extensions and proper text ad structure. For bidding strategy recommendations, see automated bidding.
