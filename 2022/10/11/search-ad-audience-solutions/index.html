Search Ad Audience Solutions: Reaching Valuable Customers

On this page

Audience Solutions OverviewBuilding AwarenessDriving ConsiderationIncreasing Customer LoyaltyAudience-Based Bidding Strategies

Audience Solutions Overview

Google's audience solutions help you reach the right users at the right moment based on their interests, behaviors, and demographics.

Building Awareness

Detailed Demographics

Target users based on marital status, education level, children's ages, homeownership status, and other advanced demographic characteristics.

Affinity Audiences

Reach people who have demonstrated a strong interest in a particular topic. These audiences are built around lifestyle interests and passions.

Driving Consideration

In-Market Audiences

Effectively drive consideration among users who are actively researching or comparing products/services like yours. These users have shown purchase intent signals.

Remarketing Lists for Search Ads (RLSA)

Reach users who have previously interacted with your website or YouTube channel. You can adjust bids for these users when they search again.

Similar Audiences

Find new customers whose behavior and characteristics are similar to your remarketing audience segments.

Increasing Customer Loyalty

Customer Match

Upload your first-party data (email lists, phone numbers, addresses) to Google Ads to reach custom audience segments across devices. Use this to:

  • Find loyal customers
  • Exclude existing customers from acquisition campaigns
  • Expand reach to users similar to existing customers

Audience-Based Bidding Strategies

Increase Bids for High-Value Audiences

When you bid higher for specific audiences, your ads may show in better positions when those users search. For example, using "Affinity Audiences," you can bid higher for users searching "mountain bikes" who are also passionate about outdoor adventure sports.

Decrease Bids for Low-Value Audiences

For audiences that perform less well, lower your bids. For example, using RLSA, you may find that certain past visitors have lower average basket sizes — reducing bids for these segments can improve overall ROI.
