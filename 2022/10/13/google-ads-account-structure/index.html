Why Account Structure Matters

Your Google Ads account structure determines how campaigns and ad groups are organized. A good structure enables:

  • Precise budget allocation
  • Accurate performance tracking
  • Effective bid management
  • Relevant ad-to-keyword matching

Structuring Principles

Principle 1: Align with Business Goals

Structure campaigns around your business objectives — separate campaigns for brand vs. non-brand, product vs. service, or different geographic regions.

Principle 2: Tightly-Themed Ad Groups

Each ad group should contain closely related keywords (5-20 keywords max). This ensures ad copy is relevant to all keywords in the group.

Principle 3: Separate by Match Type

Consider separating exact, phrase, and broad match keywords into different ad groups for better control and analysis.

Best Practices

  1. Keep it manageable: Don't create too many campaigns — aim for 5-20 active campaigns
  2. Use naming conventions: Consistent naming makes reporting easier (e.g., "Search_Brand_US_Exact")
  3. Separate budgets: Give important campaigns their own budget
  4. Don't mix match types in one ad group: Makes optimization difficult
  5. Review and restructure: Audit your structure quarterly
Related: Account structure works hand-in-hand with keyword selection and bidding strategies. Use the Performance Planner to forecast campaign performance.
