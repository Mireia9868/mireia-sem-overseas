App Promotion Overview

Google's Universal App Campaigns (UAC) are the only way to promote your app on Google Search, Google Play, YouTube, and the Google Display Network from a single campaign.

Campaign Types

TypeGoalBidding
App InstallsDrive app downloadstCPA (target cost per install)
App EngagementRe-engage existing userstCPA (target cost per action)
App Pre-registrationBuild pre-launch interestMaximize pre-registrations

How UAC Works

You provide:

  • App name (from Google Play or App Store)
  • Budget and target CPA
  • Creative assets (text, images, videos, HTML5)
  • Geographic and language targeting

Google's machine learning handles the rest — automatically testing different combinations across networks and optimizing for your target.

Best Practices

  1. Provide diverse assets: At least 4 text ideas, 4 images, and 1 video per orientation
  2. Set realistic tCPA: Start with your historical CPA, not lower
  3. Give it time: UAC needs 2-4 weeks to exit the learning phase
  4. Don't make frequent changes: Changes reset the learning phase
  5. Use creative refresh: Update assets every 2-4 weeks to combat ad fatigue
