Keyword Fundamentals

Keywords are the terms you bid on to trigger your ads. Selecting the right keywords determines who sees your ads and how much you pay per click.

Keyword Research Process

  1. Brainstorm seed keywords: Start with your product/service names
  2. Use Keyword Planner: Google's Keyword Planner shows search volume and competition
  3. Analyze competitors: Use auction insights to see who you compete with
  4. Group by theme: Organize keywords into tightly-themed ad groups
  5. Add negative keywords: Exclude irrelevant search terms

Negative Keywords

Negative keywords prevent your ads from showing on irrelevant searches. They're essential for:

  • Reducing wasted spend
  • Improving CTR (by not showing to uninterested users)
  • Improving Quality Score

Common negative keyword categories: free, cheap, jobs, download, how to, what is, vs

Keyword Quality Indicators

IndicatorWhat to Look For

CompetitionLow-Medium competition = lower CPC
RelevanceDirectly related to your product/service
Commercial Intent"Buy," "price," "discount" signal purchase intent
Top of Page BidLower = more affordable

Best Practices

  • Start with phrase match: Balance reach and relevance
  • Use SKAGs sparingly: Single Keyword Ad Groups can be hard to manage at scale
  • Review search terms weekly: Add new keywords and negatives from actual search data
  • Align keywords with landing pages: Relevance improves Quality Score
