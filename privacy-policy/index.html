Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

1. Who We Are

This privacy policy applies to mireiasem.com ("the Site," "we," "our," or "us"). The Site provides educational content and insights about digital advertising, search engine marketing, and related topics.

If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at: hello@mireiasem.com — see our Contact page.

2. Information We Collect

When you visit mireiasem.com, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Automatically Collected Data: When you browse the Site, our web server automatically logs standard information including your IP address, browser type, referring page, and date/time of visit. This data is used for site analytics and security purposes and does not personally identify you.
  • Comments: If you leave a comment on an article, we collect the data shown in the comments form (name, email, website), your IP address, and browser user agent string to help spam detection.
  • Contact Form: If you reach out via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

3. Cookies

mireiasem.com uses cookies to improve your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit.

  • Essential Cookies: These are necessary for the Site to function properly (e.g., remembering your cookie consent preferences).
  • Analytics Cookies: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the Site. Google Analytics Privacy

4. Third-Party Advertising — Google AdSense

mireiasem.com uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. As a third-party vendor, Google uses cookies to serve ads on the Site.

  • Interest-Based Advertising: Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to personalize the ads you see based on your browsing history.
  • Opt-Out: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also manage preferences via the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis
  • Google AdSense — display advertising

Review Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • Respond to your comments and inquiries
  • Monitor and analyze Site usage and trends
  • Detect and prevent spam, fraud, and abuse
  • Serve relevant advertisements via Google AdSense

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data with analytics and advertising partners as described above. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

8. Data Retention

If you leave a comment, the comment and its metadata are retained indefinitely. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics settings. Contact form submissions are retained for customer service purposes.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR, CCPA, or similar laws:

  • Access, rectification, erasure of your personal data
  • Objection to or restriction of processing
  • Data portability

To exercise these rights, contact: hello@mireiasem.com

10. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

12. Contact Us

  • Email: hello@mireiasem.com
  • Contact page: https://mireiasem.com/contact/
